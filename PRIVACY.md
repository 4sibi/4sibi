# Privacy

4sibi operates locally and does not transmit telemetry or other information to networked systems. It checks local player processes and foreground windows to restrict macro input. Settings, profiles, and error logs are stored under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\4sibi`; exports are written to locations selected by the user.

Windows startup registration is optional and controlled by the user. Disable that option before removing or moving the executable. To remove the portable app, exit it and delete the executable; delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\4sibi` only if you also want to remove saved settings and profiles.

GitHub handles website visits, downloads, and issue submissions under its own privacy policy. Avoid posting personal information in public issues.
